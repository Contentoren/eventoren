Late evening in front of a small festival stage: a dancing crowd moves under sweeping spotlights while the band plays, hands in the air and smiles everywhere. String lights glow between the food stalls at the edges, warm and teal beams cross above the audience, and the intimate venue feels completely full. Proof that the right platform fills even small stages.
