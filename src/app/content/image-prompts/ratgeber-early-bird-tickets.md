Just after sunrise in a quiet cobbled old-town lane, a young organiser in a teal jacket smooths a blank, unprinted poster onto a round advertising column with a paste brush, a bucket and a roll of further blank posters at his feet. The low amber morning sun glows at the end of the lane and catches his breath in the cool air. He stands on the right third of the frame; the left side is calm negative space of the softly lit, empty lane and pale sky.
