On a weekday afternoon in a neighbourhood café, three young members of an event team are spreading the word: one pins a blank poster to a cork board by the door, another lays a small stack of blank flyers on the wooden counter, and the third chats with the barista while holding a teal tote bag. Warm amber pendant lights hang above the counter and late sun falls through the window. The group occupies the right third of the frame; the left side is calm negative space of the softly blurred café interior with empty tables.
