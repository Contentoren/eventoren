Close, warm scene at the entrance table of a school party in a gymnasium foyer in the early evening: a volunteer door helper in his fifties wearing a deep teal hoodie carefully fastens a plain coloured fabric wristband around the wrist of a sixteen-year-old girl, who holds out her arm while her friend waits beside her. On the table lie neat bundles of blank wristbands in four colours next to a closed ID card lying face down. Amber string lights glow over the doorway. The helper, the teenagers and the table occupy the right third of the frame; the left side is calm negative space of the softly blurred foyer and a dim hall beyond with gentle warm light falloff.
